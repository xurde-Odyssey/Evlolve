# Evolve Adaptive Intelligence

Phase 6 adds a deterministic interpretation layer around the existing Phase 3 evidence and progression engines. It does not create a second level system and it does not award XP directly.

## Domains

Activities map to development domains: physical training, recovery, mental training, knowledge, skill development, deep work, or career craft. Existing activity keys remain compatible. Sleep and hydration are treated as supporting recovery signals rather than automatic progression sources.

## Evidence model

The adaptive layer consumes sustainable capability, peak capability, consistency, reliability, target relationship, gap classification, deadline evidence, and Core commitment context. Manual evidence remains valid; evidence quality changes confidence rather than deleting the record.

## Adaptive outputs

`analyzeAdaptiveIntelligence` produces activity profiles, a personal development model, development frontiers, and a structured monthly analysis. Activity profiles include trajectory, commitment difficulty, deadline behavior, confidence, and a conservative state such as building, improving, stagnating, declining, or rebuilding.

Stagnation requires mature capability, repeated samples, reliable execution, and an easy target relationship. Low consistency is not treated as stagnation. Breakthrough requires repeated qualifying evidence and strong capability movement; one exceptional session is insufficient.

## Priority and interpretation

Constraints are ranked ahead of optional strength expansion. A strong non-Core activity remains recognized while a weak Core commitment remains visible. The output may intentionally be `noIntervention` when evidence is too limited or contradictory.

Behavior remains factual and non-causal. The adaptive layer does not turn social activity, missing optional sleep/hydration data, or recommendation rejection into automatic punishment.

## Policy and privacy

Adaptive policy ownership is registered under `phase-6-adaptive-v1`. Thresholds are internal policy, not UI content. Outputs should be persisted with policy provenance when they become official weekly/monthly results. No external AI service is used; behavior, restraint, health-support, and note data remain inside the application boundary.

## Current limitation

The first Phase 6 slice is pure domain infrastructure and Profile integration. Weekly/monthly persistence of the adaptive result, personalized Boss generation, recommendation effectiveness tracking, and extended simulation catalog remain follow-up work.
